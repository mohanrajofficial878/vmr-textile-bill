V.M.R TEXTILE BILL - UPDATED VERSION

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the VMR_Textile_Bill folder and double-click index.html.
3. Each new bill starts with one item row. Click + Add Item whenever you need more rows.
4. Bill numbers are generated automatically (VMR-0001, VMR-0002, ...).
5. Customer numbers are generated automatically (CUST-0001, ...); typing an existing customer name reuses that customer's number when available.
6. The total updates automatically.
7. Business Summary shows this month's income, unique customer count, and bills this month from saved browser bills.
8. Print / Save as PDF prints the bill without the summary/actions.

IMPORTANT: Records are stored in this browser's local storage on this computer. They do not sync to other devices or browsers. Clear browser data may delete saved records. Back up important records separately.


Scan & Verify Bill:
- Each bill displays a QR code containing its bill number, date and total. Internet is needed to load the QR image.
- Use Scan QR with Camera in a supported browser, or scan with your phone and enter the bill number in Verify Bill.
- Verification checks records saved in this browser only. It is not an online/public verification service, and records do not sync between devices.
- Camera scanning generally requires HTTPS or localhost and browser camera permission.
